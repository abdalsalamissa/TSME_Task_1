Features

Everything the front desk was doing manually

Six things Mowaeed handles on its own, from the moment a patient taps "book" to the moment they walk out.

Self-service booking

A booking page that respects each doctor's real availability, buffers and room capacity — no double bookings.

Automatic reminders

SMS and WhatsApp reminders at 48h and 3h, in Arabic or English, with one-tap confirm or reschedule.

No-show analytics

See which slots, doctors and channels lose you money — and which reminder timing actually works.

Waitlist that fills gaps

A cancellation instantly pings the waitlist. Most gaps refill in under 12 minutes without a single phone call.

Patient records, secured

Encrypted at rest, role-based access and a full audit trail — built to satisfy your compliance officer.

Two-way calendar sync

Google Calendar and Outlook stay in sync both directions, so a doctor blocking time never breaks the booking page.
